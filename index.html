About Dr. Ritika

Where Ayurveda meets evidence

I'm Dr. Ritika Sahu — an Ayurvedic physician with a Master's in Public Health and a deep commitment to making traditional medicine accessible, practical, and clinically grounded for the world we live in today.

My work sits at the intersection of Ayurvedic healing and modern epidemiology — which means I don't just prescribe herbs, I understand why chronic stress, burnout, and lifestyle disorders are reaching epidemic proportions, and how ancient systems hold real answers.

I work with urban professionals, working women managing hormonal imbalances, and Indian diaspora communities worldwide who want culturally rooted, personalised care — not generic wellness advice.

FAQ

Common questions

How does an online Ayurvedic consultation work?

We meet via video call (Google Meet or Zoom). You fill a pre-consultation form beforehand so I can understand your history. The session covers your Dosha, lifestyle, and health concerns — followed by a written protocol within 24 hours.

I'm based abroad — can I still book a consultation?

Absolutely. I regularly consult with patients in the USA, UK, UAE, Canada, Singapore, and Australia. International payment methods are supported. Weekend and morning slots are available to suit most time zones.

Can Ayurveda work alongside my existing treatment?

Yes — Ayurveda is integrative, not a replacement. I always take your current medications and conditions into account. My public health background means I approach this responsibly and evidence-informedly.

Do I need to stop my current medicines?

No. I will never advise stopping prescribed medication. Ayurvedic protocols are designed to work alongside your existing treatment plan, and I'll always consider herb-drug interactions.

Which language will the consultation be in?

I consult in both English and Hindi. You're welcome to switch between both during the session — whatever feels most comfortable for you.

How do I get the Ayurvedic herbs you recommend?

I recommend herbs available on reputable Indian platforms (Amazon India, Netmeds, 1mg) or trusted Ayurvedic brands. For NRI clients, I recommend globally shipped brands or diaspora-accessible alternatives.
